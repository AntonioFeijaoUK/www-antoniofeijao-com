* 2025-03 - I have now unlocked access to more advanced 

> Hi there,
> 
> You now have access to `OpenAI o1` and `OpenAI o3-mini` in the API.
> 
> `o1` is a large reasoning model with broader world knowledge, and `o3-mini` is our new small reasoning model, optimized for cost.
> Both models support function calling, Structured Outputs, and developer messages.
> `o3-mini` can outperform `o1` in coding and other reasoning tasks.
> It’s also `93% cheaper` than `o1`, significantly faster, and supports streaming.
> 
> For both models, you can also choose between three reasoning effort options—low, medium, and high, to optimize for your specific use cases.
> 
> This flexibility allows `o1` and `o3-mini` to “`think harder`” when tackling complex challenges or to prioritize speed.
>
> In addition to the `Chat Completions API`, you can use these models in the `Assistants API` and `Batch API` today.
> 
> These models also come with a larger context window of `200,000 tokens` and a max output of `100,000 tokens`.
