## Implications

OSIRIS-REx mattered because it aimed not just to observe a small body remotely, but to bring pristine asteroid material back to Earth for laboratory study. That raised the mission's significance far beyond ordinary flyby or orbital reconnaissance. A successful sample return from Bennu promised direct evidence about the early Solar System, asteroid composition, and the kinds of materials that may have contributed to the origins of life.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2016-2021)

Within five years of launch, OSIRIS-REx had already reached Bennu, mapped the asteroid in detail, and successfully collected a sample. At that stage alone, the mission had become historically important as a technically demanding demonstration of precision navigation, asteroid operations, and contact sampling on a small body.

### By the Mid-2020s

By the mid-2020s, OSIRIS-REx had completed the core arc that made it historically significant: launch, rendezvous, sampling, and successful delivery of material to Earth in 2023. Its legacy already extended into asteroid science, planetary defence interest, curation of extraterrestrial samples, and the broader scientific value of returning material for analysis under laboratory conditions.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. OSIRIS-REx is likely to remain one of the defining modern sample-return missions and a key reference point for future asteroid exploration.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. If later missions return samples from many small bodies, OSIRIS-REx will probably still be remembered as one of the missions that helped make such operations scientifically routine and operationally credible for major space agencies.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. The launch of OSIRIS-REx may endure as a major milestone in planetary exploration, because it began the first U.S. mission to bring asteroid material back to Earth for direct study.
