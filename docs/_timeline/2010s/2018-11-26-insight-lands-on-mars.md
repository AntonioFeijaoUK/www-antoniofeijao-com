## Implications

InSight's landing mattered because it shifted Mars exploration inward. Earlier missions had taught humanity a great deal about the planet's surface, atmosphere, rocks, and water history. InSight was designed to ask a deeper structural question: how Mars formed, cooled, and evolved internally as a rocky world. That made the mission important not only for Mars, but for comparative planetology across Earth, the Moon, and the terrestrial planets more broadly.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2018-2023)

Within five years of landing, InSight had already produced the kind of results that justified its mission. It recorded marsquakes, improved knowledge of the Martian crust, mantle, and core, and gave scientists the first sustained seismic view into the interior of another planet besides Earth. That changed Mars from a world studied mainly at the surface into one whose internal structure could be inferred from direct geophysical data.

### By the Mid-2020s

By the mid-2020s, InSight had become historically important as one of the clearest examples of geophysics transforming planetary science. Its legacy did not depend on long traverses or dramatic imagery. It came from converting Mars into a seismically measured world, giving future scientists a stronger foundation for understanding how rocky planets form and evolve.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. InSight is likely to remain the defining early mission for Martian geophysics and a key reference point for future planetary seismology.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. If later missions place seismic networks on Mars or other worlds, InSight will probably still be remembered as the mission that first opened the deep interior of Mars to direct scientific study.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. InSight's landing may endure as one of the major conceptual milestones in planetary science, because it helped move exploration beyond landscapes and into the hidden internal history of another planet.
