## Implications

Raspberry Pi mattered because it made experimental computing materially accessible to far more people. Its importance was not only technical. It was economic and educational. By packaging a capable computer into an inexpensive board, it lowered the barrier to learning programming, Linux, hardware interfacing, and electronics in classrooms, homes, clubs, and hobbyist communities.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2012-2017)

Within five years, Raspberry Pi had already become one of the most influential educational computing platforms of its era. It spread through schools, maker spaces, hobbyist projects, and embedded experiments, helping to reconnect computing education with practical tinkering rather than only polished consumer devices.

### Within 10 Years (2012-2022)

Within a decade, Raspberry Pi's significance extended well beyond its original educational mission. It had become a standard platform for experimentation, prototyping, lightweight servers, home labs, robotics, and low-cost embedded systems, while also remaining an important gateway into programming and physical computing.

### By the Mid-2020s

By the mid-2020s, Raspberry Pi remained historically important as one of the clearest examples of a computing product changing both learning culture and technical practice at once. Its legacy endured in classrooms, workshops, hobby electronics, and the idea that meaningful computing education should include direct contact with real hardware and real systems.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. Raspberry Pi is likely to remain a key reference point for affordable hands-on computing education and accessible hardware experimentation.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if later educational hardware platforms become more capable, Raspberry Pi will probably still matter as one of the most successful efforts to make practical computing more reachable at scale.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. Raspberry Pi may endure as one of the products that helped preserve and democratise the culture of learning by building, programming, and experimenting directly with computers.
