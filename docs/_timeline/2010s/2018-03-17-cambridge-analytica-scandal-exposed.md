## Implications

The Cambridge Analytica scandal mattered because it made abstract concerns about data extraction, behavioural profiling, and platform power suddenly concrete for a mass public. It showed that information given up through a social platform could be recombined, analysed, and used in ways far beyond what ordinary users expected. The event did not create all of those practices, but it became the moment when many people realised how politically consequential they could be.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2018-2023)

Within five years, the scandal had already become one of the most important privacy controversies in the history of social media. It intensified scrutiny of Facebook, accelerated public and regulatory attention to political microtargeting and platform accountability, and helped push privacy, consent, and data-governance issues out of specialist circles and into mainstream democratic debate.

### By the Mid-2020s

By the mid-2020s, Cambridge Analytica remained historically important because it changed public understanding as much as policy discussion. Its legacy endured in privacy investigations, platform-regulation debates, election-integrity concerns, and the broader recognition that surveillance-based digital business models can carry democratic as well as commercial consequences.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. The scandal is likely to remain one of the standard examples used to explain how platform data misuse became a major public-policy issue.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if later privacy crises become larger in scale, Cambridge Analytica will probably still matter as an early watershed in public understanding of political data exploitation on social platforms.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. The scandal may endure as one of the moments when democratic societies began treating personal-data extraction not only as a consumer issue, but as a structural issue affecting elections, trust, and civic autonomy.
