## Implications

WannaCry mattered because it showed how quickly cyber risk can move from technical vulnerability to public crisis. The attack did not remain an abstract security event affecting only specialists. It disrupted hospitals, transport, businesses, and government services, making clear that patching failures and legacy systems could translate directly into operational harm in critical services people rely on every day.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2017-2022)

Within five years, WannaCry had become one of the defining ransomware events of the decade. Its historical importance came not only from scale, but from the way it combined exploit-driven spreading with visible operational disruption. It became a standard lesson in patch management, legacy-system risk, incident response, and the real-world consequences of underinvestment in digital resilience.

### By the Mid-2020s

By the mid-2020s, WannaCry remained a reference point for both cyber defence and public policy. It had become one of the clearest examples of how vulnerability management, healthcare resilience, and national digital preparedness are linked. Its legacy endured particularly strongly in discussions of hospitals, public-sector IT, and the cost of allowing outdated systems to remain in critical environments.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. WannaCry is likely to remain one of the central case studies in modern ransomware history because it made the operational consequences of cyber insecurity visible to a broad public.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if later ransomware campaigns become more technically advanced, WannaCry will probably still matter as the event that showed how cyber disruption could spill rapidly into healthcare, government, and international public life.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. WannaCry may endure as one of the incidents that taught networked societies that unpatched digital infrastructure can become a public safety issue, not merely a private IT problem.
