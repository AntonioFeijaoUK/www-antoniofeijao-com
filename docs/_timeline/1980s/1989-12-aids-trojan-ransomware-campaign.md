## Implications

The AIDS Trojan mattered because it introduced the core logic of ransomware long before ransomware became a large-scale criminal industry. Even though its technical methods were primitive by later standards and its campaign was distributed by floppy disk rather than over the internet, it demonstrated a durable idea: malicious software could deny access to data and then demand money in exchange for reversal.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (1989-1994)

Within five years of the AIDS Trojan campaign, the event was already recognised by security specialists as an unusual but important warning. It did not immediately create a continuous ransomware epidemic, but it established the basic extortion model that later attackers would refine once encryption, networking, and anonymous payment methods became more favourable to criminals.

### Within 10 Years (1989-1999)

Within a decade, the campaign was remembered as an early proof of concept for cyber extortion. Its historical importance grew in hindsight, because it anticipated a threat model that the technical environment of 1989 could not yet support at industrial scale.

### Within 30+ Years (1989-2019 and beyond)

More than thirty years later, the AIDS Trojan had become one of the standard origin points in histories of ransomware. It mattered not because it was technically sophisticated, but because it revealed the basic economic structure that later ransomware operators would exploit with much greater efficiency and much greater harm.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. The AIDS Trojan is likely to remain one of the central historical reference points for the early conceptual origin of ransomware.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. If cyber extortion remains a major criminal model, the 1989 campaign will probably still be remembered as an early, awkward, but historically revealing prototype.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. The AIDS Trojan ransomware campaign may endure as one of the early milestones in malware history, because it showed that digital sabotage could be turned into direct economic coercion.
