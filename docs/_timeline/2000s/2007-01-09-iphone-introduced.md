## Implications

The iPhone introduction mattered because it helped turn the mobile phone into a mainstream pocket computer with a software-centric interface, full web access, and a radically different interaction model built around multi-touch. Smartphones existed before the iPhone, but this launch changed public expectations about what a phone should be, how software should behave on it, and how mobile computing could fit into everyday life.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2007-2012)

Within five years of the iPhone's introduction, the mobile industry had been reshaped around touchscreen smartphones, software ecosystems, and mobile internet use. The device accelerated changes in application design, hardware priorities, carrier relationships, and consumer expectations about what should be possible from a handheld device.

### Within 10 Years (2007-2017)

Within a decade, the iPhone had become one of the defining consumer-technology products of the twenty-first century. Its significance extended far beyond Apple itself. It influenced the design of competing devices, the economics of mobile software, the growth of app-based services, and the migration of digital life onto phones.

### Within 15+ Years (2007-2022 and beyond)

More than fifteen years later, the iPhone remained historically important as one of the clearest turning points in the shift from desktop-centred computing to mobile-centred computing. Its legacy persisted in interface design, platform economics, media use, communications habits, and the broader expectation that a phone is a general-purpose computer people carry all day.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. The iPhone introduction is likely to remain one of the central historical markers for the mainstream arrival of modern smartphone computing.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if post-smartphone interfaces eventually dominate, the iPhone will probably remain historically important because it helped stabilise the touchscreen software model that defined mobile computing for a generation.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. The introduction of the iPhone may endure as one of the landmark product events of the digital age, because it helped change not just a device category, but the daily structure of communication, media, navigation, and computing itself.
