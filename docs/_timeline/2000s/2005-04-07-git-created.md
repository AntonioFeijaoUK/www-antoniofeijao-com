## Implications

Git mattered because it changed how software projects could be developed across many contributors, branches, machines, and organisations at once. Its distributed design made it far easier to treat version control as a shared, resilient, everyday part of development rather than a central bottleneck. That helped large open-source projects and later commercial teams work with greater speed, flexibility, and tolerance for parallel change.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (2005-2010)

Within five years of Git's creation, it had already become central to Linux kernel development and increasingly influential across wider open-source software work. Its branching, merging, and distributed workflow model gave developers a practical alternative to older centralised version-control habits.

### Within 10 Years (2005-2015)

Within a decade, Git had become the dominant version-control model for much of the software industry. Its importance extended far beyond one tool. It reshaped collaboration norms, branching strategies, code review practices, open-source contribution workflows, and the technical culture of software development itself.

### Within 20+ Years (2005-2025 and beyond)

More than twenty years later, Git remained one of the most influential developer tools in modern computing. Its legacy was visible in daily engineering practice across companies, open-source communities, educational settings, and platforms built around collaborative software work.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. Git is likely to remain one of the clearest historical examples of a developer tool that changed not only technical workflow but the social structure of software collaboration.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if future tooling abstracts away many of Git's visible mechanics, it will probably remain historically important because it helped define how distributed software development scaled globally.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. The creation of Git may endure as one of the foundational milestones in the history of software engineering, because it helped make large-scale collaborative coding practical at internet scale.
