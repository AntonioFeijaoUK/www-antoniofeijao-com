## Implications

RSA provided a practical way to use public-key cryptography for both encryption and digital signatures. That changed secure communication profoundly, because it showed that two parties did not need to share a secret key in advance in order to begin communicating securely or to verify authorship mathematically.

## Recorded Legacy

The sections below describe outcomes that are already part of the historical record and supported by the cited sources.

### Within 5 Years (1977-1982)

Within five years of its introduction, RSA had become one of the most important ideas in modern cryptography. It helped move public-key methods from theory into practical engineering and commercial interest, and it gave researchers and system designers a usable model for authentication, key exchange support, and digital signatures.

### Within 10 Years (1977-1987)

Within a decade, RSA had become a central part of the emerging modern cryptographic landscape. It influenced software security, secure communications research, and the broader understanding that mathematical methods could support trust, identity, and confidentiality in networked systems.

### Within 45+ Years (1977-2022 and beyond)

More than four decades later, RSA had become one of the foundational cryptographic systems of the internet era. Even where newer methods are preferred for speed or resilience, RSA remains historically central because it helped define how secure web traffic, certificates, signatures, and key management would be understood in practice.

## Forward Outlook

The sections below are projections rather than established history. They describe plausible future significance based on the event's recorded legacy.

### Possible Impact in 10 Years (to 2036)

Likelihood: high. RSA is likely to remain one of the main teaching examples for how public-key cryptography transformed secure communication, digital signatures, and trust on the internet.

### Possible Impact in 50 Years (to 2076)

Likelihood: medium. Even if post-quantum systems replace RSA in most operational settings, RSA will probably still matter historically as one of the first public-key schemes that ordinary systems could actually deploy at scale.

### Possible Impact in 100+ Years

Likelihood: medium. RSA may endure as one of the decisive moments when abstract number theory became part of everyday infrastructure for identity, secrecy, and verification in digital society.
